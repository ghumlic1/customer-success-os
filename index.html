What AI tools do I need?
A Claude account works: the free tier is fine to try the prompts, and Claude Pro or your company's enterprise license is best for real client data. The playbook is designed to work with Claude Chat, Cowork, and Code. Prompts can be adapted for other AI tools, but they're optimized for Claude.

Yes, and the playbook treats this as a first-class concern. There's a full governance chapter covering what you should and shouldn't upload under an enterprise AI license, a hard rule against putting PHI into any prompt, SOC 2 and BAA posture for AI vendors, a shadow-AI section on why personal consumer AI accounts are a compliance risk, and a human-in-the-loop requirement on every agent output. If your CISO or compliance team needs to sign off, you can hand them the chapter.
Does this work outside healthcare?
Yes. The playbook is written for any B2B SaaS CSM. Some governance examples reference healthcare because that's the most regulated end of the spectrum, so if your industry has lighter compliance requirements you just skip the PHI-specific sections. The agent blueprints, QBR system, stakeholder mapping, escalation playbook, and renewal frameworks are vendor and industry neutral.
